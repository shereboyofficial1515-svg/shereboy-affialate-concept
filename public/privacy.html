Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SHEREBOY AFFILIATE CONCEPT (operated by SHEREBOY TECH LTD) collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, and message content.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: your email address, when you subscribed and confirmed, and delivery records for the emails we send you. Emails are delivered through our provider, Resend.
  • Product clicks: a count of which product was clicked and when, used to show what's popular. It isn't linked to you.
  • AI assistant conversations: messages you send to the chat widget, used only to generate a response.
  • Basic technical data: standard server logs (e.g. request timestamps) used for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the Contact page.
  • To send deal updates to newsletter subscribers, if you opt in.
  • To power the AI shopping assistant's responses during your session.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the website.

3. Affiliate links

This site contains affiliate links. When you click through to a retailer, that retailer may set its own cookies and collect information according to its own privacy policy — we don't control this and encourage you to review it.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Contact and subscriber data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized administrators of this site.

5. Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link at the bottom of every email, or by contacting us. You can also request that we delete any contact information you've submitted.
